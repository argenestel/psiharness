# Commercial and enterprise use

psi is free for **personal and noncommercial use** under the [PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md):
learning on your own, hobby projects, research, teaching, and use by charities, schools, public research
organizations and government institutions.

**Commercial and enterprise use needs a separate license.** That includes using psi inside a company, offering it
to customers, building a paid product or service on it, or running it for employees or clients.

To ask about a commercial license, open an issue titled "Commercial license" at
<https://github.com/argenestel/psiharness/issues> or contact the maintainer through
<https://github.com/argenestel>.

Versions of psi published before the license change (commit `5d7ce05` and earlier) were released under the MIT
license and remain available under those terms.
