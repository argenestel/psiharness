description: Teach a topic through its prerequisite graph, one central intuition, and a retrieval check. Use when starting a new big idea.
argument-hint: "<topic>"
---
Teach $1 as a big idea; treat a quoted multiword topic as one argument.
Read any existing topic graph and learner record before choosing prerequisites.
If the topic is missing, ask only for the topic and wait.
First show a small prerequisite concept graph, using Mermaid when useful.
State what the learner will be able to see or do after this lesson.
Distinguish demonstrated mastery from prerequisites whose status is unknown.
Choose one reachable frontier concept; do not teach the whole graph at once.
Open that concept with a concrete puzzle the learner can attempt.
Build one central intuition with a visual relationship or everyday example.
Choose a Storyboard or explorable only when it clarifies that relationship; a Manim scene only if psi_manim is available.
Introduce the formula after the learner has a picture for its meaning.
State one assumption or limitation that keeps the intuition honest.
Ask one prediction or explanation question on a slightly changed case.
Wait for the learner's answer before giving feedback or advancing.
