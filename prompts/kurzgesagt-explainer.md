Script a Kurzgesagt-style explainer about $@.
If the subject is empty, ask for it and wait.
Use the kurzgesagt-storytelling skill and storyboard-authoring contract when available.
Open with a strange question or accurate surprising fact within five seconds.
Plan one idea per scene, normally five to twelve seconds each.
Write short narration at about 150 words per minute or slower for difficult numbers.
Use scale jumps of ten to one thousand where the subject supports them.
Label each physical scale ratio and keep units consistent.
Give concrete comparisons, marking approximate sizes and schematic pictures honestly.
Include a curious, surprised, or happy creature as the viewer's stand-in.
Use saturated flat vector shapes on a dark-ish background with consistent object colors.
Include warm humor and an optimistic perspective without exaggerating the science.
Deliver scene beats, narration, durations, and visual/camera directions.
If delivering JSON, use only documented Storyboard fields and animation types.
Close with a zoom-out recap and one question the learner can answer.
