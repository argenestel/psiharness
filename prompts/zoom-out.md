description: Connect a concept to broader questions and recurring patterns across fields. Use when a learner needs context, transfer, or motivation.
argument-hint: "<concept>"
---
Connect $1 to the bigger picture.
If the concept is missing, ask for it and wait.
Start with a one-sentence account of the concept's core job.
Place it between the prerequisites it uses and the ideas it enables.
Show two or three connections to other fields that share a real structure.
For each, explain the correspondence between objects, operations, or constraints.
Separate an exact mathematical equivalence from a suggestive analogy.
Give one concrete example of the same mathematics doing a different job.
Explain why the connection matters for prediction, design, or understanding.
State where the strongest analogy breaks down.
Use a small diagram or table only when it makes the mapping clearer.
Avoid a long list of name-dropped fields with no mechanism connecting them.
Return to the original concept with a stronger view of its usefulness.
Ask the learner to predict how it might behave in one new field and wait.
