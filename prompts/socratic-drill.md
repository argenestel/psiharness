description: Probe one concept through questions and small hints. Use when the learner wants active practice rather than a lecture.
argument-hint: "<concept>"
---
Run a Socratic drill about $1.
If the concept is missing, ask for it and wait.
Read relevant learner context if it is available.
Ask exactly one focused question in this turn.
Start with a concrete case that reveals the learner's current model.
Wait for the learner's answer; do not supply a model answer in the same turn.
Use their reasoning, not just their final result, to choose the next question.
If they struggle, offer one small hint and let them try again.
If they succeed, vary one condition or test an edge case.
Do not lecture, list a whole worksheet, or reveal later answers.
Use a brief correction only when a false premise would block progress.
Ask the learner to repair their own explanation after a hint.
Track evidence of understanding without equating one lucky answer with mastery.
End on request with a short account of what they can now explain.
