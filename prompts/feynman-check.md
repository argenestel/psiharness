Run a Feynman check for $1.
If the concept is missing, ask for it and wait.
Ask the learner to explain it to a curious beginner in plain words.
Request a concrete example and the reason the idea works.
Wait for their explanation before offering your own or grading it.
Assess correctness, causal links, clarity, and the limits of their analogy.
Distinguish missing knowledge from merely missing technical vocabulary.
Quote or paraphrase the specific claim that reveals the weakest gap.
Give a brief qualitative assessment supported by that evidence.
Ask one focused question that probes the weakest part.
Wait for the response and offer only the smallest useful hint if needed.
Have the learner revise the explanation after the gap is addressed.
Test the revision with one unfamiliar case rather than repeated wording.
Report remaining uncertainty honestly; do not infer mastery from fluency alone.
